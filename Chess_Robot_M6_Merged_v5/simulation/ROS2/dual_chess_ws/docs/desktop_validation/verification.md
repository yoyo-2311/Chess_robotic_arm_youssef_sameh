## Scope and limits

These initial delivery checks use simulated controllers, a mock gripper and synthetic mock vision. No real hardware, motors, external engine teammate module, camera/calibration or physical board was available or tested. No final board/mounting dimensions were invented. Every possible physical trajectory was not exhaustively executed. Subsequent [full-game validation](../full_game_validation/README.md) completed a Stockfish logic game and a short scripted robot game, and recorded the blocking motion failure during unrestricted Stockfish robot play.

Capture, castling and en passant scenario FENs and their multi-operation plans are tested with the existing Chess Brain; the GUI integration suite physically executes ordinary moves and promotion in mock simulation, not every special-move trajectory. Native RViz and its planning plugin were opened; dragging its interactive marker by hand was not automated. Gazebo verification used its headless physics server; its separate graphical viewport and contact fidelity were not validated.

Existing CAD/fixed-gripper clearance can reject legal moves. One earlier Black `e7e5` after White `d2d4` failed Cartesian clearance, and the application correctly paused without committing FEN. Passing `e2e4/e7e5` or another tested path does not establish clearance for a different board position. Revalidate geometry, TCP, all-square reach, table/support/piece collisions and actual gripper control before physical use.

Startup testing found transient planning-scene service timeouts and missed one-time status events during rapid restarts. The desktop uses a small optional motion startup delay, waits for discovery cleanup and requires recurring idle plus fresh unlocked backend safety readiness. It never retries an ambiguous motion or commits a board from an acknowledgement. A new initialization failure remains visible with controls disabled; use the explicit simulated restart after inspecting logs.

All app-managed launches and workers were closed at the end of their successful probes. Test domains were isolated and localhost-only. Runtime logs/dependencies remain ignored under `.desktop-state`/`.qt-deps`; selected evidence is copied here for review.
